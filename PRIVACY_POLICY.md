# Privacy Policy for Pocket App

**Last Updated:** July 27, 2026

Welcome to **Pocket** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal and financial information is handled securely. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application **Pocket** (Package Name: `com.asad.pocket_app`).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
When you register or sign in to Pocket, we collect personal information necessary to manage your account, including:
* **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID) obtained through Email/Password registration or Google Sign-In authentication via **Firebase Authentication**.
* **Profile Information:** Profile picture URL or display name provided via Google Sign-In (if applicable).

### B. Financial & Usage Data
Pocket is a personal finance management tool. We collect data you voluntarily enter within the application:
* **Transactions:** Expense and income amounts, transaction titles, categories, dates, payment notes, and tags.
* **Budgets:** Monthly budget limits and category targets.
* **Debts & Loans:** Debt/loan entries, borrower/lender names, amounts, due dates, and repayment records.

### C. Device & Technical Information
* **Push Notification Identifiers:** Device push tokens generated by **OneSignal** to send you budget alerts, payment reminders, and app updates.
* **Local Storage & Cache:** Offline transaction data cached locally on your device via Cloud Firestore offline persistence.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **App Functionality:** To provide, operate, and maintain your personal budget, expense, debt tracking, and transaction history.
2. **Account Management:** To authenticate your identity, secure your account, and manage user sessions.
3. **Notifications & Reminders:** To send push notifications regarding budget thresholds, debt payment reminders, or app updates via OneSignal.
4. **Data Synchronization:** To sync your data seamlessly across your devices and cloud storage using Firebase Firestore.
5. **Customer Support:** To respond to user inquiries, troubleshooting requests, and feedback.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data to third parties or advertising networks.

We share data only with trusted infrastructure service providers strictly necessary to operate the application:

* **Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore):** Used for user authentication, cloud database storage, and secure data synchronization. Refer to [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **OneSignal:** Used to manage and deliver push notifications. Refer to [OneSignal Privacy Policy](https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy).

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal and financial information:
* All data transmitted between the app and server is encrypted in transit using **TLS/HTTPS**.
* User data stored in Cloud Firestore is protected by strict **Firestore Security Rules** ensuring that only authenticated users can access their own data.
* Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is securely managed by Firebase Authentication.

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

* **Retention:** We retain your personal data and financial records for as long as your account remains active.
* **Account & Data Deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated transaction data at any time from within the App Settings or by contacting us at **support@pocketapp.com**. Upon account deletion, all personal records stored in Firebase Firestore will be permanently purged.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Pocket is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
